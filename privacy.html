Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version: We don't store your business information. What you type into Ventura AI is used only to generate your plan in that moment — nothing is saved, sold, or shared. Your payment data is handled entirely by Stripe and never touches our systems.

1. Who we are

Ventura AI is an AI-powered business plan generator operated as an independent digital product. If you have questions about this policy, you can contact us at the address listed at the bottom of this page.

2. What information we collect

When you use Ventura AI, the following information may be processed:

  • Business information you provide — the details you enter into the form (business name, description, products, pricing, customers, etc.) to generate your plan.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see, store, or process your card details.
  • Email address — collected by Stripe during checkout for your receipt. We do not have access to this unless you contact us directly.
  • Basic usage data — anonymous data such as page visits, collected via analytics tools to understand how the product is used.

3. How we use your information

The business information you provide is used only to generate your business plan in real time. It is sent directly to Anthropic's Claude AI to produce the output, and is not stored on our servers after your session ends.

We do not:

  • Store your business plan inputs in a database
  • Use your business information to train AI models
  • Sell your information to third parties
  • Use your information for advertising purposes

4. AI processing — Anthropic (Claude)

Your business information is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to generate your plan. Anthropic is one of the world's leading AI safety companies. According to Anthropic's privacy policy, data sent via the API is not used to train their models by default.

You can review Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

5. Payments — Stripe

All payments are processed by Stripe, a globally trusted payment platform used by millions of businesses. Ventura AI never sees or stores your card number, CVV, or banking details.

Stripe's handling of your payment data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy.

6. Data security

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We follow industry best practices to protect the security of our systems.

Because we do not store your business information after your session, there is no database of your inputs that could be breached or leaked.

7. Cookies

Ventura AI uses minimal cookies — only those necessary for the site to function (such as remembering your payment status during a session). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are based in the European Union, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access any personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data
  • The right to object to processing of your data
  • The right to data portability

Since we do not store your business plan inputs, there is typically no personal data held beyond what Stripe retains for payment and legal compliance purposes. To exercise your rights regarding payment data, please contact Stripe directly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Ventura AI after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
